CicloAutoNAVIGATION & SIGNALING FOR CYCLISTS

Rear light for the micro:bit V2

Turn signals, brake light and SOS on the micro:bit's LED matrix, controlled by your phone over Bluetooth. No soldering, no wiring.

Download .hex

cicloauto-rear-v2.hex · 1.4 MB
For micro:bit V2 (also runs on V1, without sound)

HOW TO INSTALL

  1. Plug the micro:bit into your computer with a USB cable. It shows up as a drive called MICROBIT.
  2. Drag cicloauto-rear-v2.hex onto that drive. The yellow LED blinks while it copies.
  3. When it's done, a dim dot blinks in the center of the screen: the micro:bit is waiting for your phone.
  4. On an Android phone, open the CicloAuto app in Chrome, tap the micro:bit icon and then Connect micro:bit. It shows ✔ and beeps.

WHAT IT DOES

FROM THE APPON THE MICRO:BIT
Turn ahead"<" or ">" arrow running to the side of the turn, with a soft "tick" on the speaker
BrakingWhole matrix lit. With a signal on, the arrow stays bright over a dimmed square
Crash / SOSWhole matrix flashing fast, plus a siren
Buttons A / BLeft / right signal by hand (A+B turns it off), for testing
Touch the logoSound on / off
